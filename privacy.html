Privacy policy

Updated 24 October 2026

Hamza FitBuddy is maintained by Hamza Imdad. For questions about your information, contact pitchppt@gmail.com. This notice describes the Android account edition and this information website.

Your diary on your device

The app stores diary entries, food and nutrition records, measurements, workouts, goals and settings on your device. You can use local diary features without signing in. Signed-in accounts use separate local diary and settings files. Signing out hides the account's local diary; it does not erase those files or its cloud backups.

Google sign-in and email codes

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides basic identity information, which may include your name, email address, profile image and Google account identifier. Supabase processes this information to create and authenticate your app account. The app uses your account ID and email to identify your account and keep its backups separate. It does not request Gmail, contacts, calendar or Google Drive permissions.

If you choose an email code, Supabase processes your email address and sends a sign-in message through Brevo. Brevo processes the recipient address and delivery information to deliver that message. Sign-in codes are for authentication, not marketing. These providers also process technical information needed to operate their services, such as IP addresses and security logs.

Optional cloud backups

When you enable cloud backup, the app encrypts your backup on the device before uploading it to Supabase. It uses an encryption password separate from your sign-in. Supabase stores encrypted backup revisions and metadata such as the owning account ID, backup identifiers, app package and timestamps. Access rules restrict normal users to their own backups. Service administrators operate the backend; those access rules do not remove administrative access to stored records.

The app can keep a protected local copy of the encryption password for automatic backups. Sign-in cannot recover a forgotten encryption password. Backups are snapshots, not live multi-device synchronisation. Keep a separate export when changing devices or updating the app.

AI, food lookup and other optional services

If you configure and use an AI provider, the meal descriptions, images and other request content needed for that action are sent to the provider you selected. Provider terms, retention and any charges depend on that provider. Signing in to Hamza FitBuddy does not provide a shared AI subscription. Food searches and barcode lookups may send search terms or barcodes to Open Food Facts. Exercise illustrations and catalogues may be downloaded from external hosting services.

Crash reporting is off by default in the F-Droid-based account build. If you enable it, Sentry can receive diagnostic events, device/app details and a support identifier; optional heartbeat reports can include the configured display name, AI-provider choice and record counts. App settings provide reporting controls. Requests routed through the project's reporting proxy are also processed by that proxy's host. Do not include passwords or private diary contents in support messages or diagnostic submissions.

Retention, removal and your choices

Local data remains until you remove it using the app or your device's controls. Uninstalling or clearing app storage can remove local diaries; export first. Cloud account and backup records remain until removed, and deleting local data does not automatically delete cloud copies.

To request access, correction or deletion of account and cloud-backup information, email pitchppt@gmail.com from the address associated with the account. Account ownership must be verified before a request is carried out. Do not send your encryption password, sign-in code or a diary export. Account deletion is currently handled through support, rather than an in-app deletion button. You can also remove Google's authorisation from your Google Account; that alone does not delete the Supabase account or backups.

Service providers and international processing

Google, Supabase, Brevo and any optional provider you use process data under their own terms and privacy notices. Infrastructure may process data in countries other than your own. The app's configured services and this policy should be reviewed before a public release.

This website and changes

These static information pages do not add analytics, advertising trackers or sign-in cookies. GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. This notice will be updated when the app's data practices change.
