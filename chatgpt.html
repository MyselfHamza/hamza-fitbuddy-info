Your MM Nutrition diary in ChatGPT

Updated 7 November 2026

Ask about your saved meals, compare recorded nutrients with your dated goals, and log meals through a conversation. MM Nutrition is maintained by Hamza Imdad. The connection is free, with no MM purchases or subscription. You need your own MM account and access to ChatGPT’s applicable connection features.

Public availability

The public ChatGPT listing is being prepared and has not been published. An existing private connection is not a public installation link. Once the listing is available, you will be able to install it in ChatGPT and connect your own MM account.

How connection works

  1. Open MM Nutrition’s listing in ChatGPT and choose Install or Connect.
  2. Sign in to your MM account on the MM connection website and approve access.
  3. Start a chat and ask, for example, “How much protein have I logged today?”

No OpenAI API key or local server is needed for this hosted ChatGPT connection. ChatGPT’s own account limits and availability apply. The optional AI Coach inside Android has a separate provider configuration.

Choose your access

Connections start read-only, with access to synced meals, nutrients and saved daily goals. Nutrition management is an optional permission: it also shares allowlisted profile fields, recorded weights and saved foods, and allows requested changes to meals, profile, weights, recipes and goals.

By default, each ordinary change needs approval on MM’s website. You can separately allow ordinary changes you request to be saved after the values are shown in chat. Deleting a meal or saved food always requires three website confirmations. ChatGPT cannot enable these permissions itself.

Manage an existing connection

Review permissions or remove access. Each connection is restricted to its signed-in MM account. The bridge uses your cloud-synced records; unsynced local meals are not available. It does not monitor meals or start background conversations.
