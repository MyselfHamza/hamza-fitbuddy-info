MM Nutrition for ChatGPT

Updated 22 November 2026

Tell ChatGPT what you ate. MM Nutrition logs it to your food diary, with calories, macros and the vitamins and minerals too. It is free, with no purchases or subscription. You need a free MM account.

Install MM Nutrition in ChatGPT

What you can do

  • Log by chat. “I had two eggs, toast and a coffee.” ChatGPT works out the portions and nutrients, shows a short summary and saves the meal.
  • Full nutrition, not just macros. Up to 54 nutrients per meal: fibre, sugars, fat types, omega-3s, every main vitamin and mineral, choline and essential amino acids. Estimates use the UK CoFID 2021 food database (2,636 foods).
  • Ask about your day. “How much protein have I had today?” “Am I low on magnesium this week?”
  • Check your goals. Compare any day with your calorie, macro and micronutrient targets.
  • Change things. Edit a portion, update saved foods and recipes, record your weight, or set new dated goals.

One account, three ways to use it

  • Android app — log by photo, voice, typed description, barcode, label scan, food search or a previous meal. Includes recipes, weight, workouts, goals and history charts.
  • Website — micronutrition.framer.website: log meals and see your dashboards in a browser.
  • ChatGPT — log and ask questions in plain words.

A meal logged in one place shows up in the others.

How to connect

  1. Open the MM Nutrition listing in ChatGPT and choose Install.
  2. Sign in to your MM account and approve access.
  3. To let ChatGPT log meals, turn on nutrition management on the same page.
  4. Start a chat: “Log my lunch: chicken wrap and an apple.”

No OpenAI API key or setup is needed. ChatGPT’s own plan limits and availability apply.

Your control

Connections start read-only. Nutrition management is optional and only you can turn it on. Before a change is saved, ChatGPT shows you the values and you approve it on MM’s website. Deleting a meal or saved food always needs three confirmations. ChatGPT cannot give itself more access.

Each connection only sees its own signed-in account. Passwords, AI keys, photos and security settings are never shared with ChatGPT. It does not watch your diary in the background. It does not diagnose conditions or manage medication.

Review permissions or remove access at any time.

Good to know

  • Nutrient values are estimates from food-composition data or the label you give. They are not lab measurements.
  • If a nutrient is not known, it stays unknown. It is never counted as zero.
  • Only meals synced to your account are visible. Unsynced offline meals appear after the app reconnects.
