Using the app

Terms of use

Updated 7 November 2026

These terms describe use of the MM Nutrition Android account edition, maintained by Hamza Imdad. Contact pitchppt@gmail.com with questions before using its account features.

A personal recording tool

The app helps record food, nutrition, measurements and exercise. Food databases and AI estimates can be inaccurate or incomplete. Review entries and verify values that matter to you. The app does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment, and its estimates should not replace professional care.

Your account and backups

Use an email address or Google account you control. Keep your sign-in codes and backup encryption password private. Only access your own records and do not interfere with other accounts or the service. Signing out hides local account data, but does not delete it. Follow the privacy policy for account-removal requests.

Account changes save to Supabase when connected. Offline changes are not stored remotely until a save succeeds. Signing in loads saved account data. Optional file exports and older encrypted backups are separate; keep a local export before an update or replacing a diary. Encryption passwords for those separate backups cannot be recovered through Google or email sign-in.

Availability and external services

The account service is being developed using free service plans. Provider quotas, outages or service changes can limit access, and continuous availability is not promised. This site does not sell a subscription. Optional AI services use your own provider configuration and may have their own fees; account sign-in does not authorise a purchase.

Google, Supabase, Brevo and other external services have their own terms. Features and these terms may change as the app develops. Material changes should be explained before they take effect. Nothing here removes any rights you have under applicable law.

Open-source software

The app builds on FitBuddy and other open-source projects. Their respective licences continue to apply. Attribution and licence details are available in the app's open-source credits.

Optional MM Nutrition connection in ChatGPT

These terms also apply to the MM Nutrition ChatGPT connection and connection/review webpage. The MM connection is free, without purchases, subscriptions or checkout. It requires your own MM account and access to ChatGPT’s applicable features; provider account, workspace and country restrictions can apply. Your ChatGPT account and optional AI-provider services have their own terms and limits.

Connect only an account you are authorized to use. Review the access request before approving it. You can keep access read-only, enable nutrition management, and separately choose faster saves for requested ordinary changes. Otherwise, ordinary changes require website approval. Deleting one meal or saved food always requires three confirmations. You can revoke access at any time through MM’s connection controls.

Review dates, portions, units and nutrition values before saving. Unknown nutrients are not zero; partial totals are incomplete. A dated weight or goal does not establish current health needs. The connection does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, adjust medication, monitor meals in the background or provide emergency care. For medical-condition targets, use your individually agreed clinical plan.

Permissions, account revision checks, short-lived approvals and provider availability may interrupt a request. Do not treat an unsaved response as confirmation of a completed change. Removing a connection does not delete an MM diary or existing ChatGPT conversations. See the privacy policy and support page for data handling and help. Nothing in these terms removes your applicable legal rights.
