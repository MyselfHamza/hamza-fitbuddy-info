Request account deletion

To request deletion of your MM Nutrition account and its saved data, email pitchppt@gmail.com from the email address associated with the account. Use the subject MM Nutrition account deletion request.

You can also start this request in the Android app under Account → Request account deletion. Support verifies account ownership before carrying out a request. Sending the request does not immediately delete the account.

What the request covers

Request removal of your authentication account and associated saved diary, nutrition entries, measurements, workouts, saved foods, goals and account settings. Mention older cloud-backup revisions if you used those features. You may instead request deletion of particular saved data while keeping your account.

Uninstalling the app, clearing its local storage or removing Google authorisation does not delete the account. After remote deletion is completed, remove any local exports and app data you no longer want to keep. Information already sent to external AI or other providers is governed by their own policies; contact those providers for their deletion options.

Keep your credentials private

Do not email passwords, sign-in codes, API keys or diary exports. If you cannot access your account email, contact support for help verifying ownership. See the privacy policy for data handling and retention information.
