02 / Your account

Choose how to sign in.

Use Google sign-in or an email code. Google sign-in uses your basic identity for authentication; it does not request access to your inbox, contacts or Drive.

03 / Your backup

Keep a recovery copy.

Cloud backups belong to your signed-in account and use a separate encryption password. They are saved snapshots, rather than live merging across devices.
