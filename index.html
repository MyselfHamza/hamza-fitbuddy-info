Android · Nutrition diary

A diary that stays yours.

Keep a food and fitness diary, review nutrition estimates, and follow your recorded activity. Use the app offline, or sign in to save and load your private account data.

Read about MM Nutrition and its Android download at the app website. Google Play publication is being prepared; this page does not claim that the app is already listed.

01 / Your records

Log, review, revisit.

Record meals, nutrition, weight and workouts. Review estimated values before relying on them.

02 / Your account

Choose how to sign in.

Use Google sign-in or your email and password, with email verification when required. Google sign-in uses your basic identity for authentication; it does not request access to your inbox, contacts or Drive.

03 / Your data

Save as you go.

Changes save to your Supabase account as you make them while connected. Sign in to load your diary, goals and history. Offline changes wait safely on your device until it reconnects.

Questions or account help?

Contact pitchppt@gmail.com for support, privacy questions or an account-deletion request. Read how data is handled and the terms of use before using the account features.
