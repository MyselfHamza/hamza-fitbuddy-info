ChatGPT connection support

Updated 7 November 2026

For help, contact pitchppt@gmail.com. MM Nutrition is maintained by Hamza Imdad. Include the action, approximate time and error text. Do not send passwords, API keys, sign-in codes, access tokens or a diary export. Remove personal nutrition details from screenshots.

Connect or manage access

The public listing is being prepared. When available, install MM Nutrition in ChatGPT, choose Connect, and sign in to your own MM account. Existing connections can review or remove access here.

If you use Google sign-in, sign into the MM website in the same browser first, then reopen the connection page. You can also use an existing MM account password. Never enter an MM password into a chat message.

Reads work, but changes are unavailable

Enable nutrition management for the intended connection on MM’s website or in the account-enabled app. Faster saves are a separate opt-in. Otherwise, open and approve the exact review link returned by ChatGPT. Deletions always need three confirmations.

A change expired or did not save

Ask for a fresh preview if your account changed or the ten-minute approval window expired. A response saying unsaved is not a completed save. Retry an uncertain tool request with the same request ID and identical details to avoid duplicates. Refresh your diary after account sync completes.

Disconnect and account removal

Remove access on the MM connections page to stop subsequent tool calls. This leaves your diary and existing ChatGPT conversations intact. ChatGPT has separate controls for its conversations. For an MM account access or removal request, contact support from your account email so ownership can be verified. See account removal and the privacy policy.
